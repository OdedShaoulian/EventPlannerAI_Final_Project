# EventPlanner AI Knowledge Base - Safety and Logistics

## Venue checks
Before confirming an event, check:
- Entrance width
- Ceiling height
- Stairs or elevators
- Available electricity
- Sharp objects or obstacles
- Wet or slippery floor
- Emergency exits
- Distance from parking to setup area

## Outdoor event checks
For outdoor events, check:
- Wind conditions
- Rain or extreme heat
- Stable ground surface
- Access to safe electricity
- Backup indoor location
- Lighting if the event is at night

## Safety recommendation
If the photo shows a narrow entrance, stairs, crowded area, or low ceiling, recommend checking the location physically or asking the client for another photo/video before confirming two dolls.

## Operator responsibility
The operator should remain close to the doll, avoid blocking exits, and coordinate movement with the event manager or DJ.
