# EventPlanner AI Knowledge Base - Packages

## Basic Package
Includes one inflatable doll for about 30 minutes, one operator, music playback from a prepared playlist, and a short entrance moment.
Recommended for small birthdays, surprise entrances, small family events, and smaller halls.

## Premium Package
Includes two inflatable dolls for about 30 minutes, one or two operators depending on the venue, music playback, and a stronger visual entrance.
Recommended for bar mitzvahs, weddings, school events, company events, and larger celebrations.

## Full Celebration Package
Includes two or more inflatable dolls, custom playlist planning, flags, timing coordination with the DJ or event manager, and a short crowd activation.
Recommended for large halls, outdoor events, sports celebrations, Independence Day events, and events with many guests.

## Choosing Dolls
For Israeli patriotic events, recommend the soldier or border police style.
For religious or Chabad events, recommend the Chabad style.
For sports fans, recommend the football or basketball styles according to the team.
For children, recommend Mickey, Minnie, Mario, monkey, or rabbit styles.

## Recommendation Rules
If the event has more than 120 guests, a Premium Package is usually more impressive than a Basic Package.
If the venue has a narrow entrance or limited space, recommend checking whether one doll is safer than two.
If the event is outdoors, check wind, electricity, weather, ground surface, access, and backup indoor option.
If the client wants a strong entrance moment for photos and videos, recommend Premium or Full Celebration.
