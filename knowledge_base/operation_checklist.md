# EventPlanner AI Knowledge Base - Operation Checklist

## Before the event
- Confirm date, hour, city, venue name, and contact person.
- Confirm whether the event is indoor or outdoor.
- Confirm available electricity, entrance path, ceiling height, and parking.
- Ask whether there is a DJ and coordinate the entrance song.
- Confirm where the doll enters from and where the operator stands.
- Prepare the selected inflatable dolls, fan/blower, extension cables, flags, and backup equipment.
- For outdoor events, check wind, weather, ground surface, lighting, and access to electricity.

## During the event
- Arrive early enough for setup.
- Check the entrance path and avoid stairs, sharp objects, and narrow doors.
- Coordinate timing with the client, DJ, photographer, or event manager.
- Keep the operator close to the doll at all times.
- Make sure the activity does not block emergency exits or create a safety issue.

## After the event
- Fold and pack equipment carefully.
- Count flags and accessories.
- Ask the customer for a review and permission to use event content for social media.
- Document any damage, missing equipment, or unusual issue.
