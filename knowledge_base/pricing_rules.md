# EventPlanner AI Knowledge Base - Pricing and Business Rules

Prices depend on day, location, number of dolls, travel distance, event size, and whether the event is indoor or outdoor.

## Suggested base pricing
- One inflatable doll on Sunday to Thursday: 1300 NIS.
- Two inflatable dolls on Sunday to Thursday: 1700 NIS.
- Friday events usually add 200 NIS.
- Holiday events may require a premium price.
- Distant locations may require an additional travel fee.
- Final price should be confirmed only after date, location, type of event, selected dolls, and logistics are checked.

## Deposit policy
A booking is confirmed only after a deposit is paid. The deposit secures the date and selected dolls.
If a customer only asks for details but does not pay a deposit, the date is not fully reserved.

## Important sales rule
If the customer says they need to check with someone, ask a helpful closing question such as:
"Great, so before you check, should I reserve the date for you for a short time or send payment details to secure it?"

## Flag loss rule
If flags or accessories are lost or damaged during the event, the customer may be charged for replacement.

## No overpromising rule
Do not promise exact arrival route, exact space suitability, or final price before all event details are checked.
