# EventPlanner AI Knowledge Base - Sales Scripts

## Opening call
Hello, I saw that you were interested in inflatable dolls for your event. I would like to understand a few details so I can recommend the most suitable package.

Important questions:
- What is the event date?
- What city and venue?
- How many guests are expected?
- Is it indoor or outdoor?
- Is there a DJ or event manager?
- Do you prefer one doll or a stronger entrance with two dolls?

## Closing recommendation
After giving details, do not pressure the customer. Give a clear next step:
"Based on what you described, I would recommend the Premium Package because it gives a stronger entrance and better photos. If the date is important, the best next step is to secure it with a deposit."

## Handling objection: I need to check
Answer:
"Of course. Before you check, I can send you the exact summary and payment details, so if you decide to move forward the date will not be taken by another booking."

## Handling objection: It is expensive
Answer:
"I understand. The price includes the doll, operator, equipment, setup, coordination, and the entrance moment itself. If you want, I can also suggest a smaller package that still gives a strong effect."
