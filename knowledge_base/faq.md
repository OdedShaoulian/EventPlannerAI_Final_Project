# EventPlanner AI Knowledge Base - FAQ

Q: How long is the performance?
A: The standard performance is about 30 minutes.

Q: Can the customer choose the dolls?
A: Yes. The customer can choose according to the event theme, availability, and recommendation from the team.

Q: Does the service include music?
A: Yes. The team can play a prepared playlist or coordinate with the DJ.

Q: Is it suitable for outdoor events?
A: Yes, but weather, wind, ground surface, lighting, and electricity must be checked in advance.

Q: What should be checked from a venue photo?
A: Entrance width, ceiling height, stairs, electrical access, open space, obstacles, emergency exits, and whether the doll can move safely.

Q: What should the assistant do when it is missing information?
A: It should ask a small number of focused questions such as date, location, number of guests, type of event, indoor or outdoor, and preferred dolls.

Q: What makes the app useful for the business?
A: It saves time, standardizes answers, helps identify logistics issues from photos, and gives sales recommendations based on internal knowledge.
